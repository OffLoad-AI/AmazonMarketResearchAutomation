The Amazon India ragi flour category generates an estimated Rs 2.85M monthly, dominated by volume leaders Aashirvaad and Vedaka in the mainstream tier, and high-margin niche sprouted products at the top end.

Coarse Texture and Gravel Particles

Inadequate sifting and coarse milling disrupt regular cooking use for everyday consumers.

Customers reward genuine taste and purity, yielding high positive review tags for Jiwa (220), Pure & Sure (88), 24 Mantra (83), TWF (80), Aashirvaad (64), and Vedaka (60).

Texture and Soft Roti Performance

Flours that produce smooth, soft rotis receive positive feedback, as seen in TWF (15 positive texture tags, soft roti mention).
